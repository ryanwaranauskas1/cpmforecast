By period

Each period is rated by CPM against the prior period, on the same tiers as a day. Changes are the median brand against itself, so they can differ from the average CPM, which rises when big spenders spend more.

The weather is set by CPM: each brand's last 7 days against its own prior 7 days, then the median brand. Clear is below 0%, Cloudy 0 to 5%, Storm 5 to 15%, Severe 15% or more. Every percentage is the median brand's last 7 days (or period) against its own prior 7 days and the same days last year, using total Meta ad account results (1-day click). Brand names and individual results are never shown.
